# Social Content Creation Skill

## Core Principles

### 1. Hook First
The first line determines whether anyone reads the rest. Weak hook = no engagement.

Hook formulas that work:
- Contrast/Surprise: `Most people do X. The ones who get hired do Y.`
- Specific number: `75% of resumes never reach a human. Here is why.`
- Painful truth: `Your resume is getting rejected before anyone reads it.`
- Pattern interrupt: `I sent 47 applications. 2 got responses. Then I fixed one thing.`

What makes a bad hook:
- Starts with "I" as first word on LinkedIn
- Vague opener: "Resume writing is important..."
- Self-promotional: "RankResume.pro can help you..."

### 2. Structure: Hook -> Insight -> CTA

LinkedIn format:
[HOOK 1-2 lines]
[LINE BREAK]
[BODY 3-5 short punchy lines]
[LINE BREAK]
[CTA - Link in the first comment.]

Instagram format:
[HOOK 1 strong first line]
[2-4 short punchy lines]
[5-8 hashtags]
[Link in bio]

### 3. Specificity Beats Generality

Bad: "ATS systems filter many resumes"
Good: "Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human sees them"

Bad: "RankResume.pro helps you optimize your resume"
Good: "Paste the job description, see your ATS match score in 30 seconds"

### 4. Caption Quality Checklist

Before accepting a generated caption:
- First line works standalone as a hook
- Contains at least one specific number, stat, or concrete detail
- Does NOT start with a brand mention
- CTA is clear and low-friction
- No corporate jargon (leverage, synergy, holistic approach)
- Platform URL rule respected
- Length is within platform target (LinkedIn 150-250 words, Instagram 60-100 words)

### 5. Image Brief Guidelines

Image and caption should NOT repeat each other - they make different points.

Image headline rules:
- Max 6-8 words
- Punchy standalone statement, not a caption summary
- Action-oriented: "Beat ATS in 30 seconds" not "ATS Resume Optimization"
- Can include a statistic: "75% of resumes never get read"

### 6. Quality Score Dimensions (0-2 each, 10 total)

- Hook strength: Does first line stop the scroll?
- Specificity: Are there concrete details vs vague claims?
- CTA clarity: Is the next step obvious and low-friction?
- Length fit: Is it right size for the platform?
- Authenticity: Does it avoid buzzword soup?

Threshold: Score < 6 means flag for regeneration.
